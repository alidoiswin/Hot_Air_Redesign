Fly Across Melbournes Botanic Gardens

Description

Drift over Melbourne on a hot air balloon flight over the Royal Botanic Gardens. Whether you're celebrating a special occasion or simply seeking a unique adventure, this serene journey combines natural beauty with magic.

Safety Info

Your safety is our highest priority.

All flights are conducted in strict accordance with regulations set by the Civil Aviation Safety Authority. Our experienced pilots are fully licensed and trained in hot air balloon operations.

Flights may be rescheduled if conditions are not ideal. Your safety always comes first.

Passengers receive a full safety briefing before takeoff, including landing procedures and what to expect during the flight. Our balloons and equipment undergo regular maintenance and safety inspections to meet the highest aviation standards.

We recommend wearing comfortable clothing and enclosed shoes for your flight. If you have specific health concerns or mobility limitations, please let us know in advance. Your adventure is designed to be not only breathtaking, but also safe, professional, and unforgettable.
