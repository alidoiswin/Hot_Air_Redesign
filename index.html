Locations

Melbourne

Drift above the vibrant cityscape of Melbourne as the sun rises, casting golden light over iconic landmarks and winding streets.
Hot air ballooning here offers a rare and quiet escape from the urban buzz, with sweeping views of parks, the Yarra River, and the skyline coming alive in the early morning calm.

Yarra Valley

Float gently over the rolling vineyards and misty hills of the Yarra Valley, one of Australia’s premier wine regions.
As the morning sun breaks through the fog, you’ll enjoy breathtaking views of lush farmland, wineries, and distant mountain ranges. It’s an unforgettable blend of serenity and natural beauty.

Geelong

Experience the coastal charm of Geelong from the sky, where the bay meets the land in a stunning display of blues and greens.
Hot air ballooning here reveals panoramic views of the waterfront, surrounding countryside, and the peaceful rhythm of this seaside city as it wakes to a new day.
